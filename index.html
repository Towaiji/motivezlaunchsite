Why Motivez

Built by three friends tired of "idk, what do you wanna do?"

Motivez started in Toronto in 2025 with three friends who were tired of watching good plans disappear into group-chat indecision. We built one place to discover nearby activities, settle the choice with the Motivez Dice, and organize the hangout with friends.

Motivez is built for students and young adults in Toronto and the GTA who want to turn "let's hang out" into an actual plan, fast. Discover what's nearby, roll the Motivez Dice when no one can decide, and coordinate with friends in one place.

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

How Motivez works, what you can discover, where we're launching, and how it compares with other event and social apps.

What is Motivez?

Motivez is a mobile app for discovering things to do and turning group-chat ideas into actual plans. It brings nearby places, events, saved ideas, friend invites, and planning tools into one place, so your group can spend less time debating and more time doing something together.

How does Motivez help a group make plans?

Start by browsing nearby activities or saving places your group might like. Once you have an idea, you can invite friends, coordinate the details, and keep the plan connected to your calendar instead of spreading everything across search, maps, polls, and a long message thread. If nobody wants to make the final choice, the Motivez Dice can choose from your saved options.

How does the Motivez Dice work?

The Motivez Dice is for the moment when everyone is interested but nobody will pick. Save a few places or activities, roll the Dice, and it selects one of those options at random. Your group keeps control of the shortlist while Motivez settles the final decision.

What kinds of things can I discover on Motivez?

Motivez is being built to surface nearby events, food spots, activities, casual hangouts, and local places worth trying. You will be able to narrow ideas by factors such as mood and budget, whether the plan is coffee after class, a free afternoon activity, dinner with friends, or a night out.

Who is Motivez for?

Motivez is designed first for university students and young adults, but you do not need to be a student to use it. It is for anyone who wants an easier way to find something nearby, make a decision with friends, and organize a casual plan without turning it into a project.

Is Motivez free?

Joining the Motivez early-access waitlist is free, and the app is planned to be free during early access. If paid features are introduced later, pricing and what is included will be explained clearly before anyone is charged.

Is Motivez available now, and where is it launching?

Motivez is currently accepting early-access signups and is coming soon to iOS and Android. The first launch is focused on Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area so the discovery experience can be useful and locally relevant from day one. Expansion to other Canadian cities is planned as the app grows.

How is Motivez different from Eventbrite?

Eventbrite is mainly built around finding, registering for, and buying tickets to scheduled events. Motivez is focused on the broader decision and planning process with friends, including places and casual activities that may not require a ticket. You might use Eventbrite when you already know you want an event; Motivez is for figuring out what the group should do and getting the plan organized.

How is Motivez different from Meetup?

Meetup is useful for discovering communities, public groups, and recurring gatherings around a shared interest. Motivez is designed more for making plans with people you already know, especially when the group needs ideas or cannot agree on one. The focus is quick local discovery, choosing an option, and coordinating the hangout.

Is Motivez a dating or nightlife app?

No. Motivez is a social discovery and planning app, not a dating app, and it is not limited to nightlife. It is meant for everyday plans such as coffee, food, parks, museums, campus hangouts, events, and nights out with friends.

How should I use Motivez safely?

Motivez is designed around discovering public places and planning with friends, but every real-world outing still requires good judgment. Check venue details before leaving, meet in public when appropriate, tell someone where you are going, arrange a safe way home, and do not rely on an app listing as a guarantee that a place or event is suitable for you.
