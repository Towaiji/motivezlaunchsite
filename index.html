Motivez FAQ

What is Motivez?

Motivez is a mobile social discovery and planning app that helps students and young adults find things to do nearby, discover local events and places, and plan hangouts with friends.

Is Motivez for students?

Yes. Motivez is built for university students and young adults who want to make plans faster and spend less time deciding what to do.

Is Motivez like Eventbrite?

Not exactly. Eventbrite focuses on ticketing and event registration. Motivez focuses on social discovery and planning with friends across local activities, places, and events.

Is Motivez a nightlife app?

No. Motivez is not nightlife-only. It supports daytime and evening plans, student activities, and general local social discovery.

How is Motivez different from Meetup?

Meetup is centered on communities and recurring groups. Motivez is designed for quick, friend-first social planning and finding what is happening nearby right now.

Is Motivez free?

Early access for Motivez is free to join.

Is Motivez safe?

Motivez is designed for friend-based social planning and local discovery. As with any social app, users should follow standard safety practices and meet in public places.

Does Motivez work in Toronto?

Yes. Motivez has an initial focus on Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.
