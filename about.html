About Motivez

Motivez is a social discovery and planning app for university students and young adults in Toronto and the GTA. We help you turn "idk, what do you wanna do" into an actual plan.

Why We Built It

Built by three friends tired of "idk, what do you wanna do?"

Motivez started in Toronto in 2025 with three friends who kept running into the same problem: everyone wanted to go out, but nobody wanted to spend an hour searching, comparing options, and forcing the group to choose.

We built Motivez to put discovery and planning in one place. Find nearby events and places, filter by mood and budget, use the Motivez Dice when the group is stuck, then invite friends and keep the plan organized without a long chain of messages.

We are building Motivez in Toronto with direct feedback from the students and young adults who will use it. The goal is simple: less time deciding, more time actually doing something together.

What We Are

Social discovery and planning: nothing else

What Motivez is

  • A social discovery and planning app for students and young adults
  • Location-based discovery of things to do nearby
  • Motivez Dice: randomly picks a saved place when you can't decide
  • Social planning with friends and shared calendars
  • Budget-friendly filtering for every plan

What Motivez is not

  • Not a dating app
  • Not a nightlife-only app
  • Not a ticket marketplace like Eventbrite
  • Not a recurring-interest-group platform like Meetup
Our Mission

Never be bored again

Our mission is to make it effortless for students and young adults to turn "let's hang out" into an actual plan, fast. Discover what's nearby, let the Motivez Dice settle the debate, and keep everyone on the same page.

Motivez is built for university students and young adults in Toronto and the GTA, anyone who's ever sat in a group chat watching a plan slowly fall apart.
